# NYC Paranormal Activity Report 1984

Normalized Markdown transcription of the municipal field report. Each section
below is an addressable knowledge unit: cite it as
`sources/nn/nyc-paranormal-activity-report-1984.md@## <Heading>`.

## Market Demand

Reported paranormal incidents in the five boroughs rose from 34 in 1982 to 211
in 1984, a 520% increase over two years. Manhattan accounts for 61% of all
reports, concentrated in pre-war high-rise buildings above the 40th floor.

Commercial property managers are the fastest-growing source of call-ins,
overtaking private residences in Q3 1984. Insurers have begun writing
paranormal-damage riders, which establishes a payer for remediation work.

## Response Times

lead_agency:: Ghostbusters
median_response_minutes:: 47
target_response_minutes:: 30

Median response time across all 1984 incidents was 47 minutes, measured from
dispatch call to on-site containment start. The Office of Emergency Management
recommends a 30-minute target for class-V entities in occupied buildings.

## Containment Capacity

Existing storage facilities operate at 78% of rated capacity. At the current
incident growth rate, capacity is exhausted within 14 months without expansion.
This is the single binding constraint on service volume.

## Public Safety Findings

No civilian fatalities were recorded in 1984. Three equipment-related injuries
occurred among operators, all during class-V containment. Protective equipment
standards have not yet been codified by the city.
